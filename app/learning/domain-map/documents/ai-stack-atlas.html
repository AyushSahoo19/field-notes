One request, all the way down

The layers aren't just a taxonomy — they're a call stack. Here is what happens between typing a question and getting an answer, and which stratum each step belongs to.

  1. L15 → L17 · APP Your text hits an SDK, gets a system prompt, tool schemas, and conversation history attached.
  2. L16 · RETRIEVAL The query is embedded, searched against a vector index plus BM25, fused, reranked; the top passages are pasted into the context.
  3. L13 · SERVING A gateway routes to an engine. The tokenizer splits your text into ~4-character pieces. The scheduler slots you into a continuous batch with 200 strangers.
  4. L12 · WEIGHTS ~100–700 billion parameters are already resident in HBM — loaded once, shared by every request on that machine.
  5. L08 → L07 · ARCHITECTURE Prefill: your whole prompt runs through every layer at once — attention, MoE routing, FFN, RMSNorm, residual add — writing a KV cache as it goes.
  6. L04 · KERNELS Each of those operations dispatches a fused CUDA/Triton kernel that tiles the matrices to fit in on-chip SRAM, because HBM is 100× further away than registers.
  7. L02 · SILICON Tensor cores execute the matmuls in FP8, accumulating in higher precision. Billions of MACs per token.
  8. L03 · FABRIC If the model is sharded, partial results cross NVLink between GPUs — an allreduce inside the forward pass itself.
  9. L00 · PHYSICS Roughly a joule of electricity per few tokens becomes heat, is carried off by a cold plate, and leaves the building as warm water.
  10. ↑ BACK UP Decode emits one token at a time, each pass re-reading the KV cache. Tokens stream out through the gateway, get logged and traced at L20, and arrive as text — where an agent at L17 may decide it now needs to call a tool, and the whole descent begins again.
Built as a map, not a syllabus — you are not meant to learn this top to bottom. Pick the layer your work sits in, then read one layer down to understand your constraints and one layer up to understand your users.

Every layer is a full career. Entries reflect the ecosystem as of mid-2026; the fast-moving layers (12, 13, 17) drift in weeks, the slow ones (00, 05, 07) barely move in decades.
